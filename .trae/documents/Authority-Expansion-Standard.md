# Authority Expansion Standard – whyclaimdenied.com

## OBJECTIVE
Transform whyclaimdenied.com from “State Hub + Reason Pages” into a Layered Authority Insurance Resource Platform:

- Layer 1 – State Hub
- Layer 2 – Reason Pages
- Layer 3 – Intent-Based Blog
- Layer 4 – Case Examples
- Layer 5 – Future SaaS Appeal Tool Integration

## SCOPE
Applies to every current and future state. Never publish a state without this full stack.

## URL CLUSTER MODEL (KEEP EXISTING URLS)

### Existing (preserve)
- State Hub: `/auto-insurance-claims-denied-{state}/`
- Reason Page: `/auto-insurance-claims-denied-{state}/{reason-slug}`

### New Blog Structure (mandatory)
`/blog/{state}/`
- AUTO POSTS
  - `/blog/{state}/why-was-my-auto-insurance-claim-denied/`
  - `/blog/{state}/how-to-appeal-denied-auto-insurance-claim/`
- HEALTH POSTS
  - `/blog/{state}/why-was-my-health-insurance-claim-denied/`
  - `/blog/{state}/how-to-appeal-denied-health-insurance-claim/`
- CASE POSTS
  - `/blog/{state}/case-example-auto-claim-denial-appeal-success/`
  - `/blog/{state}/case-example-health-claim-denial-appeal/`

## CONTENT LENGTH
- Blog posts: 1,400–2,000 words
- Case posts: 1,200–1,800 words
- Insurance = YMYL; depth & clarity mandatory.

## CONTENT TEMPLATES

### “Why Was My Claim Denied” Posts
1. Emotional opening (humanized)
2. State-specific insurance context
3. 6–8 most common denial reasons (each with: short explainer, real-world example, what-to-do, link to full reason page)
4. Immediate next steps
5. Appeal overview
6. Link back to state hub
7. CTA for appeal tool (future SaaS placeholder)

### “How to Appeal” Posts
1. Clear intro
2. Step-by-step appeal process (state-specific)
3. Required documents
4. Deadlines
5. Department of Insurance contact info
6. When to escalate
7. Links to denial reason pages
8. Link to state hub

### Case Example Posts
1. Scenario overview (anonymized)
2. Type of denial
3. Why insurer denied
4. Mistake or issue
5. Appeal process taken
6. Outcome
7. Lessons learned
8. Related links (hub + reason + appeal post)

## EDITORIAL RULES
- Reference real denial patterns
- Mention state-specific regulations & DOI
- Include consumer rights
- Human tone; no filler/AI fluff
- Cite statistics & external sources
- Never copy-paste; always original

## INTERNAL LINKING – CIRCULAR AUTHORITY MODEL
Each blog post must link to:
- 5–8 related reason pages
- State hub
- Related blog posts
- Case examples

Each reason page must add:
“Need more context? Read our full {State} auto denial guide” → blog post

Each case page must link:
Hub + Appeal post + Relevant denial reason page

## SEO INTENT LAYERING
Target categories:
- Informational: “why was my claim denied {state}”
- Problem-focused: “insurance denied my claim what now {state}”
- Action-oriented: “how to appeal denied claim {state}”
- Emotional: “insurance denied my accident claim help”
- Case curiosity: “example of insurance claim denial overturned”

## AD-FRIENDLY LAYOUT
- Short paragraphs (2–4 lines)
- Clear H2/H3 segmentation
- Lists & bullets
- Section breaks every 300–400 words
- Natural ad zones between sections
- Maintain readability

## CASE COLLECTION
- Research real-world denials
- Anonymized storytelling
- If inspired by published case → add “Source inspiration: [link]”
- No plagiarism

## SITEMAP & TECHNICAL
- Append-only updates to `sitemap.xml` (never remove existing URLs)
- Add Blog nav link after “About Us”
- Ensure blog category & case pages indexed
- Validate canonical tags
- Auto-update legal pages with new structure

## FUTURE STATE PROCEDURE (AUTOMATED)
When adding any new state:
1. Create state hub
2. Create 5–7 reason pages
3. Create 2 auto blog posts
4. Create 2 health blog posts
5. Create 2 case posts (1 auto, 1 health)
6. Implement circular linking
7. Update sitemap
8. Validate canonicals & internal linking integrity

No state will ever be published alone again.

## QUALITY GATE (BEFORE PUBLISH)
- Readability check
- Internal links verified
- Outbound references verified
- Factual accuracy
- No duplicate phrasing across states
- State-specific adaptation confirmed
- No bulk automation without validation

This document is the permanent protocol—merge with State-Adding Prompt & Sitemap Updating Skill and apply automatically.
